Privacy

Back

Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Football Quiz only stores what it needs to run the leaderboards.

What is stored on our server

  • The name you choose for the boards (2–16 characters). Use a nickname if you like.
  • A random player id made by the app the first time you open it. It isn't linked to your phone, email or anything else; it just stops other people posting under your name.
  • Your scores and answers: which questions you answered, whether you got them right, your score and when you played. Answers are used for the boards and to find questions that are too easy, too hard or wrong.
  • Question reports, if you send one: the question, the reason you picked and your note.
  • Notifications, only if you switch them on: the push address your browser gives us (a long random web address at Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft that lets us send to this browser and nothing else) and which notifications you want. Switch them off in the Me tab and it's deleted straight away; it's also deleted when your browser tells us it's no longer used.

That's everything. We don't collect your email, location, contacts or device details, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, your record, the questions you've seen, your daily-quiz history and your player id are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing the site's data removes them.

Fonts and other services

The fonts are part of the app, so it never contacts Google or any other third party. The question bank and the app itself work offline. If you switch notifications on, they're delivered through your browser's own push service (for example Google's for Chrome); the messages are encrypted so only your browser can read them.

Deleting your data

To have your name, scores and answers removed, open an issue at github.com/teo2301/football-quiz/issues with the name you play under. You don't need to give any other details. We'll delete it and close the issue.
